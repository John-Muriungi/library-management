Borrowing Terms and Conditions
1. Borrowing Period

The standard borrowing period is 14 days. You may select a due date up to 30 days from today.

2. Renewals

Books may be renewed once if no other member has reserved them. Renewals must be requested before the due date.

3. Late Returns

Late returns incur a fine of $5 per day per book. Maximum fine is limited to the replacement cost of the book.

4. Lost or Damaged Books

You are responsible for the care of borrowed books. Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at the current market value.

5. Library Privileges

Members with overdue books or unpaid fines may have borrowing privileges suspended until the matter is resolved.

6. Returns

Books must be returned to the library during opening hours. Do not leave books outside the library.

By borrowing this book, you agree to all terms and conditions stated above.
